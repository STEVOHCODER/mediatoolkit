About MediaToolkit

MediaToolkit is a free online toolkit for everyday document and image work: compress a PDF before emailing it, merge invoices, convert a spreadsheet to CSV, format JSON, turn Word files into PDF — all without installing software or creating an account.

How it works

  • PDF, image and Office tools run on secure servers through our processing provider (iLoveAPI), the same engine family behind services processing millions of files a day. Files are deleted automatically after processing.
  • Data tools (JSON, CSV, Excel, Word→Text/HTML) run entirely in your browser using JavaScript libraries — your data never leaves your device.

How it's funded

The site is free to use and supported by advertising. We aim to keep ads non-intrusive and clearly separated from the tools.
