Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

1. Files you process

PDF, image and Office tools: your files are uploaded to our server and forwarded to our processing provider (iLoveAPI) so the requested conversion can run. Processed files are deleted automatically — our server does not keep copies after you download the result (temporary results expire after 30 minutes), and the provider deletes files about two hours after processing.

Data tools (JSON, CSV, Excel, Word → Text/HTML): these run entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device.

2. Data we do not collect

No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics profiles. We do not read, mine or sell the contents of your files.

3. Cookies

We store only your theme preference (light/dark) in your browser's local storage. If advertising is enabled, our ad partner (Google AdSense) may use cookies as described in Google's advertising policy. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Logs

Our hosting provider keeps standard web-server logs (IP address, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention. Tool runs are rate-limited per IP address; request counts are kept in memory and discarded within minutes.

5. Your rights

Because we keep no user accounts or file copies, there is nothing to export or delete on request. Contact us with any privacy question and we will answer within a reasonable time.
